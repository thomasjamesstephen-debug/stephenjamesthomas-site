The Story

I'm an AI-first creative director and filmmaker specializing in cinematic, social-native advertising. I blend generative AI with traditional craft to build bold visual worlds — fast — from concept through finished spot, without sacrificing taste or story.

My work focuses on character, tone, and spectacle, using AI as a creative accelerant rather than a gimmick. I have a background in film, design, and high-volume branded content. I help brands and agencies move quicker, think bigger, and ship work that actually looks finished.

The path here is unusual on purpose: USC MFA in Film & Television, a Cal Poly degree in Animal Science (yes, really), and a degree in Media & Graphic Design from Art Institute LA. Three lenses for the same problem — story, system, surface. Nine years at R&CPMK and MediaNug directing concept, edit, and design for global brands and theatrical campaigns. Now an AI Filmmaker at Jiffy Shirts, shipping ~40 AI ads a month with full media-buying attribution attached.

If you need someone who can sit in the room with a Hollywood producer in the morning and a SoFla restaurant owner in the afternoon and ship work that respects both — that's the job I want.
